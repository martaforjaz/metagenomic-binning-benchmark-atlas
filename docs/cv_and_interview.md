# CV statements and interview explanation

## Two English CV sentences

Developed a provenance-aware metagenomic binning benchmark resource with 63 historical dataset-subset records, 1,008 traceable field assertions, automated validation and reproducible scientific figures.

Implemented and verified a common-protocol AMBER re-evaluation of two archived public binning outputs, with pinned dependencies, input checksums, a technical report and a searchable static catalog.

These statements describe completed artifact development and re-evaluation, not personal execution of the binners, exhaustive literature screening or a novel algorithm. The extension used AI coding/research assistance, documented in provenance.md. Pages activation is separate from static-site implementation.

## Approximately 60-second explanation

“My undergraduate research project started with a practical problem: metagenomic binning papers often use different datasets and evaluation protocols, which makes their scores difficult to compare. I extended the original spreadsheet into a resource that records where each claim came from and what still needs checking. It contains 63 historical dataset subsets, with separate layers for assemblies, references and uncertainty. The audit caught issues such as ambiguous units and shared accessions that do not necessarily identify the same subset. I also implemented a small reproducible evaluation: I fetched two official archived outputs for the same CAMI sample, verified their input identities and recalculated their metrics with one AMBER protocol. The result illustrates a purity-completeness trade-off, not a global winner. The main contribution is an inspectable workflow connecting evidence, data validation and scientific communication. The literature coverage is deliberately bounded, and I clearly separate the 2024 work from the independent extension.”
